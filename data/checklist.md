# Sunrise Bakes Kitchen Opening and Closing Checklist

Use this checklist for each baking day. Mark items only after checking them;
pause production and resolve any food-safety concern before continuing.

## Opening

- [ ] Confirm today's orders, item counts, pickup appointments, and courier
  windows; flag any item that has sold out.
- [ ] Check that the work area, trays, utensils, and food-contact surfaces are
  clean and sanitised.
- [ ] Wash hands; tie back hair; put on a clean apron. Keep pets and household
  activity out of the kitchen during preparation.
- [ ] Check ingredient labels and use-by dates, especially for packaged
  chocolate, fillings, and seasonal ingredients.
- [ ] Keep allergen-containing ingredients sealed and separate where practical;
  use clean utensils and wash hands between tasks. Shared-kitchen
  cross-contact remains possible.
- [ ] Preheat the oven and check that the thermometer and scales work.
- [ ] Label each order with its item, quantity, and customer name; keep delivery
  details with the packed order, not on public display.
- [ ] Tell customers promptly if an ingredient delay or small-batch shortage
  affects a confirmed order.

## Closing

- [ ] Reconcile orders against pickups and courier handovers; message customers
  about any missed collection.
- [ ] Record leftovers, sell-outs, and ingredient shortages for the next bake.
- [ ] Cool, wrap, and store any retained food according to its recipe and
  storage needs; do not leave perishable fillings at room temperature.
- [ ] Label and date opened or prepared ingredients; refrigerate or discard
  them as appropriate.
- [ ] Wash, rinse, and sanitise equipment and food-contact surfaces; sweep and
  mop the floor.
- [ ] Empty rubbish and recycling; check the fridge and freezer doors are shut.
- [ ] Turn off the oven and other appliances; check taps are off and the kitchen
  is safe before leaving.
- [ ] Update tomorrow's order list and note any customer follow-up.
