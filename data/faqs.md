# Sunrise Bakes FAQs

## 1. Where are you located?

We are a home bakery in Tampines, Singapore. Pickup is by appointment, and we
share the exact block and collection instructions after confirming an order.

## 2. How far in advance should I order?

Please order standard items at least 48 hours ahead. Custom cakes need at least
7 days and a confirmed quote. A sourdough loaf ferments for at least 24 hours,
but please still use the 48-hour order lead time.

## 3. Is there a minimum order?

Yes, $30 (SGD) before delivery. A neighbour collecting a spare same-day item may
sometimes be an exception, but please check first; it is not a pre-order.

## 4. Do you deliver outside Tampines?

Islandwide courier delivery may be available. The fee is separate and depends
on the address and date. We offer a 2-hour window, not an exact arrival time.

## 5. Can I walk in and buy something?

We do not have a storefront, so please arrange pickup first. Occasionally we
have extra bakes, but popular items can sell out by late morning.

## 6. Are seasonal items always available during their listed months?

No. Durian cream puffs are usually made June–August and pineapple tarts
November–January, but ingredients and batch sizes vary. Festive pineapple tarts
often sell out early.

## 7. What allergens are in the bakes?

The item-by-item list is in [allergens.md](./allergens.md). Our shared kitchen
handles wheat, milk, eggs, soy, peanuts, tree nuts, and sesame, so cross-contact
is possible and we cannot guarantee an allergen-free item.

## 8. Can you make a custom cake?

We can consider a custom cake with at least 7 days' notice. Send the date,
serving size, and design idea for a quote; the order is not confirmed until we
agree on the details and receive the deposit.

## 9. What happens if I need to cancel?

Cancel at least 72 hours before pickup or dispatch for a full deposit refund.
At 48–72 hours, the deposit becomes 60-day credit. Inside 48 hours, missed
pickups, and dispatched orders are not refundable. If we cancel, you receive a
full refund.

## 10. Can I request an exact delivery time or leave the order at the door?

We can offer a 2-hour window, but courier traffic and rain mean we cannot
promise an exact time. Please arrange for someone to receive the order. The
courier may leave after trying to contact you; a second attempt may cost extra.
