# Sunrise Bakes FAQs

## Do you accept custom cake orders?

Yes. Please place an order at least 72 hours in advance.

## Can you guarantee an allergen-free product?

No. The kitchen is shared, so cross-contact is possible.

## What should I do if I have a serious allergy?

Speak directly with bakery staff before ordering.

## Can I ask whether an item is available?

Yes. Availability may change throughout the day.
