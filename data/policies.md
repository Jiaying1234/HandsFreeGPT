# Sunrise Bakes Order and Delivery Policy

Sunrise Bakes is a small home bakery in Tampines, Singapore. Orders are subject
to availability and are confirmed only after we reply with a confirmation and
receive the required deposit.

## Ordering and lead times

- For standard menu items, order at least 48 hours ahead. Sourdough needs at
  least 24 hours of dough fermentation, but the normal 48-hour order lead time
  still applies.
- Seasonal items are made in small batches and should be pre-ordered; they can
  sell out before the listed seasonal window ends.
- Custom cakes are not on the everyday menu. Ask at least 7 days ahead for a
  quote; design, size, and date must be agreed before the order is accepted.
- The minimum order is $30 (SGD) before delivery. Seasonal boxes count toward
  the minimum. We may make an exception for a neighbour collecting an available
  same-day extra, but that is not a confirmed pre-order.
- A 50% deposit is due when we confirm an order. The balance is due by pickup
  or before dispatch.

## Pickup and delivery

- Pickup is by appointment in Tampines; the exact block and collection
  instructions are shared after confirmation. Please do not arrive early without
  checking, as this is a home kitchen.
- Islandwide delivery may be available by third-party courier. The fee is
  quoted separately based on the address and date, and must be accepted before
  the order is confirmed.
- Choose a 2-hour delivery window. The courier route, rain, and traffic can
  affect arrival time, so an exact arrival minute cannot be promised.
- Please provide a working phone number and arrange for someone to receive the
  order. If nobody answers, the courier may leave after contacting you; a
  second delivery attempt may incur another fee.

## Changes and cancellations

- Changes are subject to availability. Please request them at least 48 hours
  before pickup or dispatch.
- Cancel at least 72 hours ahead for a full deposit refund.
- For cancellations 48–72 hours ahead, the deposit is retained as credit for a
  future order, valid for 60 days.
- Cancellations less than 48 hours ahead, missed pickups, and dispatched orders
  are not refundable because ingredients and baking time have already been
  committed.
- If Sunrise Bakes has to cancel, we will offer a full refund, including any
  delivery fee already paid.

## Allergies and product issues

Please read [allergens.md](./allergens.md) and tell us about allergies before
paying. We bake in a shared kitchen and cannot guarantee an allergen-free item.
If an item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, message us with a photo
within 24 hours so we can review it.
