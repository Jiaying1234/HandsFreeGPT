# Sunrise Bakes Menu

Sunrise Bakes is a small home bakery in Tampines, Singapore. Prices are in
Singapore dollars (SGD) and are for pickup orders; delivery is charged
separately. Items are baked in small batches and are available while stocks
last.

## Everyday bakes

| Item | Price | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Country sourdough boule | $12 | One 700 grams loaf; allow at least 24 hours for the dough |
| Kaya coconut buns | $10 | Box of 4; made with house-style kaya, not individually wrapped |
| Garlic herb focaccia | $14 | One tray; cut into 6 pieces |
| Butter croissant | $4.80 | Each; best eaten on the day of pickup |
| Pandan gula melaka muffin | $4.50 | Each; coconut topping |
| Cinnamon roll | $6 | Each; cream-cheese drizzle packed separately |
| Dark chocolate sea-salt brownie | $5 | Each; fudgy centre |
| Lemon drizzle loaf | $5 | Per slice |

## Seasonal (limited batches)

| Item | Price | Availability |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Durian cream puffs | $16 | Box of 4, usually June–August; pre-order recommended |
| Pineapple tarts | $18 | Box of 8, usually November–January; festive batches often sell out early |

Seasonal dates depend on ingredient supply, and a batch may sell out before the
end of its stated window. Message before paying to check the day's availability.
