   - ai answer: The price of a Country sourdough boule is $12 (SGD) for one 700 g loaf.

2. Do cinnamon rolls contain wheat?
   - correct answer: Yes. Cinnamon rolls contain wheat/gluten, milk, and eggs.
   - ai answer: Yes, cinnamon rolls contain wheat as one of their main ingredients.

3. Can Sunrise Bakes guarantee an allergen-free product?
   - correct answer: No. The shared kitchen handles several allergens, so cross-contact is possible and no item can be guaranteed allergen-free.
   - ai answer: No, Sunrise Bakes cannot guarantee an allergen-free product. They operate in a shared kitchen that handles wheat, milk, eggs, soy, peanuts, tree nuts, and sesame, so cross-contact is possible.

   - ai answer: You should place a custom cake order at least 7 days in advance and provide a confirmed quote.
